Fleet strategy · concentrate & isolate vs distribute clean

The fleet concentrates its unavoidable radiological cost at one isolatable, remotely-sited breeder, while distributing clean, low-neutron burners to the point of use. Radiological burden is centralized and contained; generation is clean and distributed — the fleet is ~10–15× cleaner in neutron terms than a mainstream D–T tokamak, and net-favourable versus the incumbents it displaces on carbon, land, waste longevity and safety.
