The independent-library check that anchors this board

The 3-D neutronics ladder was re-run start-to-finish on the local node with ENDF/B-VIII.0 — a different evaluated nuclear-data library than the cloud campaign's FENDL-3.2 — on the real spherical-tokamak geometry. The two libraries agree on the 3-D ceiling to a few parts in a thousand. Same physics, two codepaths, two data libraries, one answer. Deposits: 10.5281/zenodo.21746157 · 10.5281/zenodo.21746479. The reduced-order engine passes its frozen-anchor regression 19/19 on the same node — the identical suite on the validation page.
