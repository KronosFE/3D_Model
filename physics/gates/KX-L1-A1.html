The reproduce-first rule

A gate integrates into the interactive 3-D model only after it reproduces on the verification chain — same physics, independent hardware, and (where nuclear data is involved) an independent evaluated library. Cloud-scale gates join the moment their results survive that check. Nothing on the model asks for trust.
